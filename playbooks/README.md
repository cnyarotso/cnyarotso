# Analyst Playbooks

These playbooks document repeatable reasoning rather than environment-specific button clicks.

## Contents

- [Alert triage](alert-triage.md): move from alert to scoped disposition.
- [Threat-intelligence enrichment](threat-intelligence-enrichment.md): preserve provenance, confidence, age, and local relevance.
- [Detection validation](detection-validation.md): test data quality, expected matches, false positives, and operational readiness.

## Standard decision record

Every investigation should record:

1. What happened and when.
2. Which evidence supports the statement.
3. What is observed, inferred, or unknown.
4. Why it matters to this asset, identity, or business service.
5. The decision, confidence, owner, and next action.
6. How containment or remediation will be validated.
